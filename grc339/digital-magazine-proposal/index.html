Introduction

365 Living

This magazine is a lifestyle magazine focused on navigating life in your 20s, from journalistic entries about identifying yourself, to practical advice on designing homes and building healthy habits. It would include topics such as guides on designing your personal life and living spaces, and inspiration to take into daily life.
